About the Platform

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) National Weather Intelligence Project

Mission & Vision

The National Weather Big Data Analytics Platform integrates observational station streams, INSAT-3DR satellite imagery, Doppler weather radars, historical climate databases, and AI machine learning models to deliver unified, real-time weather intelligence to government decision-makers and Indian citizens.

Early Disaster Warning

Mitigate loss of life through rapid cyclone track and flash flood predictions.

Data-Driven Governance

Equip central, state, and district authorities with synchronized risk metrics.

Agricultural Support

Provide farmers with micro-climate rainfall advisories for crop protection.
